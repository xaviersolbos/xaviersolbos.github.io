Sense comptes d'usuari

Dades guardades al teu dispositiu

Quan jugues amb altres persones, l'app es connecta al nostre servidor de jocs per posar-vos en contacte. S'hi envien el teu sobrenom, el codi de la sala i les jugades de la partida. Aquestes dades només serveixen per fer funcionar la partida i no es fan servir per a res més. Si no jugues en línia, no s'envia res al servidor.

L'app mostra anuncis amb Google AdMob. Per mostrar-los, Google pot recollir dades del dispositiu com l'identificador de publicitat, l'adreça IP i informació tècnica del mòbil, i fer-les servir per mostrar anuncis i mesurar-ne el funcionament.

  • Pots canviar la teva decisió quan vulguis des de l'enllaç «Privacitat dels anuncis», al final de la llista de jocs.
  • També pots restablir o desactivar l'identificador de publicitat als ajustos d'Android (Google → Anuncis).

Compres dins l'app

L'opció de treure els anuncis es paga a través de Google Play. El pagament el gestiona Google; nosaltres no rebem ni guardem les teves dades de pagament.

Menors

L'app no està adreçada específicament a menors de 13 anys i no en recull dades personals de manera conscient. Si creus que un menor ens ha facilitat dades, escriu-nos i les eliminarem.

Els teus drets

Pots demanar accedir a les teves dades, rectificar-les o suprimir-les escrivint a fma.corp.app@gmail.com. També pots presentar una reclamació a l'Agència Espanyola de Protecció de Dades (aepd.es).

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Android app 42 Jocs (package com.fma.tabletopclassics) uses and why.

Who we are

FMA · Contact: fma.corp.app@gmail.com

No user accounts

The app does not ask you to sign up and does not ask for your real name, email or password. It only asks for a nickname to play with, which you can make up.

Data stored on your device

Your nickname, language, sound and music settings and saved games are stored only on your phone. We do not send them anywhere. They are deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Online games

When you play with other people, the app connects to our game server to put you in touch. Your nickname, the room code and the moves of the game are sent to it. This data is only used to run the game and for nothing else. If you do not play online, nothing is sent to the server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show them, Google may collect device data such as the advertising ID, IP address and technical information about your phone, and use it to show ads and measure how they perform.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the first time you open the app we ask for your consent using Google's form. You can accept or refuse personalised ads.
  • You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy" link at the end of the list of games.
  • You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The option to remove ads is paid through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to fma.corp.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will say so on this page with a new update date.
